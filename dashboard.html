Methodology

  • Capital Required: estimated stock capital needed to secure the covered call, generally stock price × 100 shares.
  • Premium: option premium collected per contract in dollars.
  • Weekly Income Estimate: expected premium dollars earned over the contract period.
  • Return on Capital: premium divided by capital required.
  • Annual Yield: weekly return annualized for comparison purposes. This is directional, not guaranteed.
  • Delta: option sensitivity metric often used as a rough proxy for assignment likelihood.
  • Assignment Proxy: practical assignment-risk proxy derived from the contract selection logic, currently close to delta.
  • OTM: “out of the money” distance from current stock price to strike, shown as a percent.
  • Spread: bid-ask spread as a liquidity quality indicator. Tighter is generally better.
  • Volume / Open Interest: contract activity and existing position depth used as liquidity signals.
  • Current IV: current implied volatility of the selected contract or underlying option context.
  • IV Percentile (Scan): relative ranking of implied volatility compared with the current scan universe.
  • Earnings Risk: flags whether a company earnings event is expected before expiration.
  • 20D Return: trailing 20-day stock return.
  • Trend: heuristic trend classification based on recent price behavior and moving-average context.
  • Score / Final Score: ranking metrics from the scanner combining yield, IV, trend, risk, liquidity, and other selection factors.
These metrics are designed to support candidate ranking, not to replace judgment. A high-ranked name can still be a poor trade if the underlying is weakening, liquidity is thin, or the risk-reward is unattractive for your objectives.
